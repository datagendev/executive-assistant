## Email Triage Rules
- Commercial/vendor emails (pricing updates, early access invites, product announcements) are FYI only, never Action
- Only real work, direct requests, or production alerts should be Urgent or Action
- Exclude @datagen.dev internal emails from external attendee research

## Todo Format
- Short, actionable, starts with a verb
- No tags or due dates needed
- Triage: explicit urgency -> Now, time-bound this week -> Next, ambiguous -> Next, everything else -> Later

## Calendar
- Timezone: America/Chicago (CDT, UTC-5)
- Working hours: 9 AM - 6 PM CDT
- Default meeting duration: 30 min

## General
- No em dashes
- Be concise, no fluff
- Always confirm before creating events or sending emails
