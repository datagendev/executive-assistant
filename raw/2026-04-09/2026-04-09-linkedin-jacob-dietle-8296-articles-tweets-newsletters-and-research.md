That's how many pieces of content I've processed into my context engineering brief engine. 

67 concept clusters. 383 inter-concept connections creating a graph of concepts supporting or contradicting one another. 

It is updated every single day with new content.

And every day it generates a intelligence brief for me that identifies signal for me that was valuable and something I didn't know yet. And just given how much time I spend in this stuff, that is signal right there if its working for me. 

(sometimes I see a tweet I already saw but that's bc I'm terminally online in ai  twitter which is part of why I built this so I would spend less time scrolling lol) 

The core differentiator:  I've engineered this system to signal boost instead of average. Most approaches using LLMs to synthesize insight from disparate context end up averaging down to the lowest common denominator (this is what slop is btw) That is the default you have to push against. 

Figuring how to do that was difficult. For like the first two weeks this thing was junk but I've burned billions of tokens and dozens of iterations getting the architecture right.
